## Zephaniah 1:2–3 (id: 26156)

God's judgment will remove things in the reverse order of their creation ([Genesis 1:20–26](https://ref.ly/Gen1:20-Gen1:26)). People and animals are mentioned first, then birds, and finally fish.

This order shows that judgment is a reversal of creation, moving backward through what God originally made.

## Zephaniah 1:5 (id: 26159)

* Scripture condemned the worship of heavenly bodies like the sun, moon, and stars (see [Deuteronomy 4:15–19](https://ref.ly/Deut4:15-Deut4:19); [2 Kings 21:3](https://ref.ly/2Kgs21:3); [23:12](https://ref.ly/2Kgs23:12)).
* Milcom was the national god of the Ammonites. In some Old Testament passages, Milcom is closely connected with Molech, a pagan deity linked with child sacrifice. Because of this connection, some texts use these names in overlapping ways.

    The law clearly forbade the worship of Molech and related practices ([Leviticus 20:1–5](https://ref.ly/Lev20:1-Lev20:5)). Even so, these forms of worship continued to tempt Israel, especially through contact with neighboring nations ([2 Kings 16:3](https://ref.ly/2Kgs16:3); [21:6](https://ref.ly/2Kgs21:6)).

* **Associated Passages:** Zephaniah 1:5

* **Associated ACAI Concepts:** LORD (ID: `deity:Lord`); Molech (ID: `deity:Molech`); Swear (ID: `keyterm:Swear`)
